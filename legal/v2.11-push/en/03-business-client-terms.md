## 11. Ranking and absence of preferential treatment

The main parameters affecting venue visibility, in their usual order of relative importance, are:

1. relevance to the query and selected filters, and geographical proximity;
2. activity, technical availability and moderation status of the profile;
3. completeness and currency of the profile;
4. availability of a valid Programme or offer.

Relevance to the query and selected filters, and geographical proximity, carry the greatest weight because they directly reflect the user’s request and the practical accessibility of the venue. Activity, technical availability and moderation status follow because unavailable or restricted profiles should not occupy leading positions. The completeness and currency of the profile, followed by the existence of a current programme or offer, make the result more useful but do not usually displace relevance to the query and accessibility.

The precise weight of the parameters may change to protect against manipulation and improve relevance. A plan may include visibility in map or discovery features, but does not guarantee a specific position, number of impressions, routes, visits or revenue.

OwnCup currently does not offer its own cafés, beverages or rewards that compete with Client offers, and does not provide related businesses with preferential access to data or ranking. Paid position enhancement, if introduced, will be clearly labelled and disclosed before use.

The Client may offer its goods and services through other channels on different terms. OwnCup does not impose price parity or exclusivity.
