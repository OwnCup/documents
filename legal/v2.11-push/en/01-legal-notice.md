## 2. Role of OwnCup

OwnCup provides a digital customer engagement and retention platform for independent cafés and small networks. Through the platform, participating cafés may publish a profile and offers, operate loyalty programmes and receive aggregated activity indicators, while Guests may find participating cafés, record confirmed visits and redeem rewards. Specific functions depend on the plan.

In the legal documents, **Client** means the party to a business agreement with OwnCup, and **Location** means a specific verified physical place of business operated by the Client. One Client may operate one or more Locations. In the user interface, a Location may be called a café or venue.

The single OwnCup service is provided through the marketing website, the **app.owncup.eu** web application and native applications for iOS and Android. These surfaces perform different functions, but are provided by the same Operator and governed by one consistent set of legal documents.

OwnCup is launched in stages on a country-by-country basis. A particular country is treated as an active market when it is expressly identified as available in the relevant interface, app store listing or OwnCup offer. Technical availability of the service or the existence of a translation does not, in itself, constitute the launch of the offering in that country.

OwnCup is not intended for the listing or promotion of goods, services or materials that are available only to adults or designated as **18+**. In particular, the service may not be used to advertise alcoholic products, tobacco or nicotine products, gambling, pornography or sexual services.

Each participating café independently:

- offers goods, services, promotions and rewards;
- establishes the specific rules of its Programme within the OwnCup rules and applicable law;
- is responsible for the accuracy of published information, the availability and quality of goods and the actual provision of rewards.

OwnCup is responsible for providing and operating the digital platform to the extent established by the published terms and mandatory law.

For a Client-initiated push campaign, OwnCup is the technical sender, applies uniform rules for determining the Recipients, frequency and sending time, and may review the Material. The Client does not select specific Recipients and does not receive their personal or technical identifiers. Automatic reminders about visits, progress and rewards in a particular café's programme are generated by OwnCup under uniform platform rules; the café does not control the trigger or the specific Recipient of such a reminder. OwnCup is not the seller or provider under the Client's offer; the allocation of responsibility and personal-data roles is set out in the **Business Client Terms of Service**, **Marketing Push Messaging Policy** and **Privacy Notice**.
