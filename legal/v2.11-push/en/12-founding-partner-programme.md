# Founding Partner Programme

**Version 2.11-PUSH-EN.**  
**Publication and effective date: 1 September 2026.**

## 1. Purpose

A programme limited in participant numbers and time for Business Accounts that are among the first to join OwnCup. Its purpose is to build up a density of participating cafés at launch (see the phased city launch strategy), rather than permanently change the plan structure.

Participation is not reserved by an oral promise. Entitlement arises only after confirmed activation and if a place is available in the relevant track; the exact terms are communicated to the Client before activation.

## 2. Two independent tracks

The tracks are counted separately from each other. Participation in one does not create entitlement to the other.

### 2.1. Freemium 150

The first 50 Business Accounts to join OwnCup (by the date of first activation of the Business Account, regardless of the plan selected when joining) receive an increased Freemium limit of **150 unique active Guests per calendar month** instead of the standard 100 (**Business Client Terms of Service**, §6; **Pricing & Plan Terms**, §2 and §5) for the entire period during which that Business Account remains on Freemium.

The increased limit is attached to the particular Business Account (billing owner), not to the plan or location: if the Account temporarily moves to a paid plan and subsequently returns to Freemium, the increased limit applies again.

### 2.2. Start for €9.99 per month or €99.90 per year in the first year

The first 50 Business Accounts to take out the paid Start plan receive it at a special price of **€9.99 per month excluding VAT** with monthly billing or **€99.90 for the first 12 months excluding VAT** with annual billing, instead of the standard €14.99 per month or €150 per year. The special price applies for the first 12 months from payment activation.

After the first 12 months, the special price ends. The next period does not begin automatically and may be ordered separately at the then-current standard price, unless OwnCup agrees otherwise in writing.

## 3. Counting places

A place is counted in chronological order of confirmed activation, one place per Business Account. For track 2.1, confirmed activation means that OwnCup has activated the Business Account; for track 2.2, it means OwnCup’s confirmation of the Start order and receipt of payment where required. Once the 50 places in the relevant track have been filled, subsequent Business Accounts receive the standard **Pricing & Plan Terms** without entitlement to this benefit.

## 4. Non-transferability and no artificial accumulation

The benefit is attached to the particular Business Account and may not be obtained repeatedly by the same owner or a related person creating a new Account. The provisions of §2 of the **Business Client Terms of Service** on duplicate and artificially separated Accounts apply to attempts at circumvention.

## 5. Amendment and termination of the Programme

OwnCup may close the Programme to new participants at any time without stating reasons and without a separate notice period – this is not an amendment of an Agreement already in force, but discontinuation of the offer for persons that have not yet joined. Terms already granted under this Programme remain in force and are not cancelled retroactively, except in cases of confirmed fraud or artificial circumvention of limits (§2 and §6 of the **Business Client Terms of Service**).

## 6. Relationship with the standard Terms

This Programme provides an additional benefit to a limited number of initial participants. Its priority is limited to the special terms expressly stated in this document. All other provisions relating to plans, payment, VAT, upgrades and downgrades apply without change (**Pricing & Plan Terms**, §§1–12).
